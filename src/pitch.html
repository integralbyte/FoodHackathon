Published evidence

Student food insecurity

GAO estimates 23% of U.S. college students experienced food insecurity in 2020, using the National Postsecondary Student Aid Study.

Published evidence

Salt and bitterness

A 2019 sensory study found about 30% lower bitterness with NaCl in simple chicken broth models containing L-tryptophan. This is not a Sorami taste test or a recommended salt dose.

Sodium, but not potassium, blocks bitterness in simple model chicken broths ↗
Published evidence

Yeast extract

A 2024 cell-based study reported at least 40% inhibition of seven bitter taste receptors with an AMP-rich yeast extract. Receptor response is not the same as human-perceived bitterness.

Modulation of bitter taste receptors by yeast extracts ↗

Product design targets

Formulation & preparation

85% sorghum + millet, 47g of grains per cup, non-fried processing, and a five-minute boiling-water rehydration target are proposed product attributes. The pore illustration has no calibrated diffusion coefficient, temperature model, or measured cooking-time prediction. The supplied Umami Chicken packaging is visual concept art: its 78g net weight, 11g protein, 400mg sodium, and gluten-free wording have not been validated. The pitch calculations retain their original 47g grain input and are not a nutrition calculation for this package.

Published drying research

Microwave-vacuum drying

A 2022 fermented-rice-noodle study reported porous noodles and two-minute hot-water rehydration after microwave-vacuum drying. Its formulation and results are not measurements of Sorami. The chamber and drying slider are qualitative illustrations.

Rice-noodle drying study ↗
Published hydration research

Thin strands and water paths

A wheat-noodle study tested diameters from 0.80 to 3.00mm at 100°C and related water sorption to time divided by diameter squared. It supports shorter hydration paths, not a measured five-minute result for Sorami. Pore size and accessibility both matter.

Water Sorption Kinetics of Wheat Noodle with Different Diameters ↗
Team-proposed manufacturing flow

Manufacturing & nutrition

The revised proposal uses sorghum flour, foxtail millet flour, tapioca starches and hydrocolloids; exact ratios remain open. The proposed route is steam-assisted extrusion, a 24-hour hold at 4°C and hot-air/microwave-vacuum/hot-air drying. Each 78g cup contains a 55g dried noodle block, 10g seasoning and 13g toppings. The settings, aw ≤0.60, five-minute preparation and resistant-starch retention are targets for validation. Whole-cup nutrition values remain team targets; the final formulation needs analysis.

Illustrative economics

Price, margin & pantry cups

Default: $3.99 shelf price − $1.20 store share − $1.60 production cost = $1.19 remaining. The pantry option allocates cost ÷ 20, initially $0.08, leaving $1.11. This excludes overhead, distribution, taxes, waste, and program delivery costs. Negative scenarios remain visible. The $6.50 premium comparison is a deck benchmark, not a current market survey.
