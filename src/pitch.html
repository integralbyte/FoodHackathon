Published evidence

Salt and bitterness

Our proposed seasoning uses sea salt. A separate 2019 study found about 30% lower bitterness with NaCl in model chicken broths containing L-tryptophan. It did not test our sea salt or Sorami.

Yeast extract

A 2024 cell-based study reported at least 40% inhibition of seven bitter taste receptors with an AMP-rich yeast extract. Receptor response is not the same as human-perceived bitterness.

Published drying research

Microwave-vacuum drying

A 2022 fermented-rice-noodle study reported porous noodles and two-minute hot-water rehydration after microwave-vacuum drying. Its formulation and results are not measurements of Sorami. The chamber and drying slider are qualitative illustrations.

Published hydration research

Thin strands and water paths

A wheat-noodle study tested diameters from 0.80 to 3.00mm at 100°C and related water sorption to time divided by diameter squared. It supports shorter hydration paths, not a measured five-minute result for Sorami. Pore size and accessibility both matter.

Team-proposed manufacturing flow

Manufacturing & nutrition

The revised proposal uses sorghum flour, foxtail millet flour, tapioca starches and hydrocolloids; the team’s newly supplied recipe percentages total 100% and remain proposed. The proposed route is steam-assisted extrusion, a 24-hour hold at 4°C and hot-air/microwave-vacuum/hot-air drying. Each 78g cup contains a 55g dried noodle block, 10g seasoning and 13g toppings. The settings, aw ≤0.60, five-minute preparation and resistant-starch retention are targets for validation. Whole-cup nutrition values remain team targets; the final formulation needs analysis.

Illustrative economics

Price, margin & pantry cups

The new costs, launch and packaging scenes adapt Sorami_Cost_Model_v9.xlsx for Spicy Garlic only. The second-flavour changeover and four per-flavour tests are removed; all other rates and the full $30,400 start-up allowance are retained. Estimated costs are $1.89 pilot and $0.77 scale; planned price is $3.49. The workbook’s retail scenario deducts 30% retailer margin, 13% distributor margin and 18% trade promotions in sequence. Gross profit is before overhead, tax and pantry support. Pantry allocation is cup cost ÷ 20, with extra program costs excluded. Full source cells, channel assumptions, cash needs, inventory constraints and packaging limits are available in the new scenes’ Details.
