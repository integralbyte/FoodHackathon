# Cornell University seal

Unmodified transparent PNG published by Cornell University Human Resources.

- Source page: https://hr.cornell.edu/hr-partners/tools-resources/style-guide
- Asset: https://hr.cornell.edu/sites/default/files/2022-12/cornell_logo-small.png
- Retrieved: 7 November 2026.
- Native dimensions: 222 × 222. The original PNG is embedded unchanged for offline viewing and displayed at its original aspect ratio. This is Cornell's published seal-only variant, without the separate university-name wordmark.

The seal sits immediately after “home.” in the launch heading. The word and seal stay together when the heading wraps on narrow screens. Text and seal have separate reveals so the image does not inherit a clipping wipe. It identifies the proposed home-campus launch context and is not labelled as an existing partner, sponsor or confirmed distribution agreement. The rest of the timeline is unchanged.
