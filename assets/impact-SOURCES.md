## Farmers

The requested 38.5 tonnes is calculated on the latest proposed recipe basis: 49% sorghum + 21% foxtail millet = 70% of the 55g noodle recipe = 38.5g grain flours per cup. At 1,000,000 cups, 38.5g × 1,000,000 / 1,000,000 = 38.5 metric tonnes. The main panel displays “per 1 million cups.” This is an ingredient-demand scenario, not actual purchases, a verified finished-product analysis, or a farmer partnership. The supplied sorghum gram-weight discrepancy remains in recipe Details.
