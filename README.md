# Sorami

Sorami is a sorghum and millet cup-noodle concept developed for a food innovation hackathon. This website presents the product through an interactive story, connecting food research with product design and business planning.

The experience moves from a shelf of competing noodles to a cup that comes apart into its grains. It then explores the recipe, manufacturing process, nutrition, costs, campus launch and proposed support for farmers and food banks.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SVG, the demo includes:

- Scroll and keyboard navigation through animated scenes.
- A procedural cup-to-grain animation and product close-ups.
- Nutrition, ingredient and cost diagrams.
- Desktop and mobile layouts with reduced-motion support.
- Embedded images and video for offline viewing.

Product figures and manufacturing settings are proposed targets. Research sources and assumptions are documented within the demo.
